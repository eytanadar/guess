## Ecology

No data files: the recipes generate food webs with `makeNicheModel` (the niche model of Williams and Martinez, 2000) and fetch observed interactions with `makeFromGloBI` (Global Biotic Interactions, globalbioticinteractions.org; cite GloBI and the original sources).

## Epidemiology

| file | source | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `school-contacts-SIMULATED.gdf` | **simulated** for the cookbooks (seeded); its structure follows published wearable-sensor studies of primary schools (mostly within-class contact, less within a grade, little between grades) | 246 pupils in ten classes and their ten teachers; `role`, `room` (the class), `grade`; edge `weight` = minutes of contact in a day. Never use as data. |
| `outbreak-cases-SIMULATED.csv`, `outbreak-transmissions-SIMULATED.csv` | **simulated** for the cookbooks: a branching process with overdispersed (negative binomial) offspring, R = 1.6 falling to 0.6 when gatherings are banned on day 28 | a line list (Id, onset, age, ward) and the traced transmissions (Source = infector, Target = case, setting); about 12% of infectors untraced. Never use as data. |

Both are regenerated by `make_epidemiology.py`. The travel recipe uses `world-airports.gdf` (above).
